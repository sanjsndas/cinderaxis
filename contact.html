    Before you write

    How quickly do you respond to emails? +
    We aim to reply within 2–3 business days. For urgent editorial deadlines, please mark your subject line with “URGENT”.
    Do you accept guest posts or sponsored content? +
    We occasionally accept carefully selected guest contributions. Sponsored content is clearly labelled and limited. Please contact partners@cinderaxisliving.com for details.
    Can I visit the studio? +
    Our London studio is open by appointment only. If you'd like to visit, please email us with a brief note about the purpose of your visit.
    How do I pitch a story idea? +
    Send a concise pitch (no more than 200 words) to editors@cinderaxisliving.com. Include a working title, a short summary, and why it fits our editorial focus.
