    • About Us

    We write about the spaces that shape us.

    Cinder Axis Living is an independent editorial publication covering interior design, architecture, materials, lighting, and modern living. We publish in-depth guides, critical essays, and practical advice for people who care about how buildings and interiors shape daily life.

    Our story

    Founded in 2021, Cinder Axis Living began as a small newsletter for architects and designers who wanted more than glossy imagery. We wanted to understand why certain spaces feel right — and how to recreate that feeling with intention.

    Today, we publish long-form articles, interviews, and practical guides that bridge the gap between professional practice and everyday living. We are reader-supported and proudly ad-free.

    What we cover

    Our editorial scope spans residential and commercial interiors, architectural theory, material innovation, lighting design, space planning, furniture, and the cultural forces that influence how we live.

    Whether you're renovating a single room, commissioning a new build, or simply curious about the built environment, our aim is to give you clear, honest, and useful information.

    Our mission

    We believe that good design is not a luxury — it's a fundamental part of a well-lived life. Our mission is to demystify the design process, celebrate thoughtful architecture, and equip our readers with the knowledge to make better decisions about the spaces they inhabit.

    “A home should offer choices. Some days you want to be together; some days you need to be apart — without leaving the building.”

    — Elena Marsh, Editor-in-Chief

    Editorial policy

    • Independence: We do not accept payment for coverage. Sponsored content, if any, is clearly labelled.
    • Accuracy: We fact-check our articles and cite sources where appropriate. If we make a mistake, we correct it promptly.
    • Originality: All content is original and produced by our editorial team or commissioned contributors.
    • Respect: We treat the people we interview and the subjects we cover with respect and fairness.
    • Transparency: We disclose any potential conflicts of interest in our articles.
